## Milestone Tips

**Keep milestones playable.** Each milestone should produce something a person can actually try. "I finished the enemy behavior" is not a milestone -- "enemies appear in the level and the player can die" is.

**Front-load the hard parts.** If you are unsure whether a feature will work, build it in Milestone 1. Finding out in Milestone 3 that your core mechanic does not feel fun is too late.

**Dates are commitments to yourself.** Missing a milestone date is a signal to cut scope, not to extend the deadline. Move features to a later milestone or cut them entirely.

**Use your milestones for playtesting.** At each milestone, have someone else play your game. Their feedback is more valuable than your own because you already know how it works.

---

## Quick Milestone Checklist

Before marking a milestone done, confirm:

- [ ] The game launches without errors
- [ ] All features listed for this milestone are functional
- [ ] The game is playable from start to some kind of ending
- [ ] No crashes during normal gameplay
- [ ] You have tested it on a fresh launch (not just from where you left off mid-session)
- [ ] At least one other person has tried it and given feedback
