## Example: Filled-In Template

**Level Name:** Crystal Caverns

**Level Theme/Setting:** Underground cave with glowing crystals

**Target Difficulty:** Medium

**Estimated Play Time:** 2-3 minutes

**Difficulty Curve Style:** Sawtooth

### Section 1
**Layout:** Flat cave entrance, wide open, a few small gaps in the floor with crystals visible below. The path goes right with gentle downward slopes.
**Pacing:** Rest
**Difficulty:** Easy
**New Mechanic:** None
**Enemies/Hazards:** None — this is the calm intro
**Collectibles:** 5 coins along the main path to guide the player forward

### Section 2
**Layout:** The cave narrows and the path goes downward via a series of ledges. At the bottom, a wide cavern opens up with platforms suspended over a dark pit.
**Pacing:** Action
**Difficulty:** Medium
**New Mechanic:** Crumbling platforms (fall after you stand on them)
**Enemies/Hazards:** 2 bat enemies that fly in figure-eight patterns. Crumbling platforms over the pit.
**Collectibles:** A health crystal hidden on a ledge to the left that requires backtracking slightly

### Section 3
**Layout:** A tall vertical shaft going upward. Walls on both sides with alternating ledges. A large crystal at the top glowing brightly (the goal).
**Pacing:** Action
**Difficulty:** Hard
**New Mechanic:** Combines crumbling platforms with wall-jumping from earlier levels
**Enemies/Hazards:** Bats circling in the shaft. Some ledges are crumbling platforms.
**Collectibles:** The large crystal at the top is the level completion collectible

### Enemies and Hazards Summary
| Type | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| Cave Bat | Flies in a repeating figure-eight pattern, damages player on touch |
| Crumbling Platform | Looks like a normal platform but shakes and falls 1 second after the player steps on it, respawns after 4 seconds |
| Dark Pit | Bottomless pit, player dies and respawns at last checkpoint |

### Win Condition
Reach the large glowing crystal at the top of the vertical shaft in Section 3.

### Mechanic Teaching Map
**Mechanic:** Crumbling platforms
**Introduce:** First crumbling platform appears over solid ground — if it falls, the player just lands safely below
**Develop:** Three crumbling platforms in a row over the pit, spaced so the player must keep moving
**Twist:** Crumbling platforms placed on the walls of the vertical shaft, combined with wall-jumping
**Conclude:** The final stretch has crumbling platforms, bats, and wall-jumps all together

---

> **Tip:** You don't need to fill every field perfectly. Even a rough sketch gives AI much more to work with than starting from nothing. Fill in what you can, then refine as you playtest.
