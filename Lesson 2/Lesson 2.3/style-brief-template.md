# Lesson 2.3 Template: Game Style Brief

## Overview

This is your personal style brief template. Fill it out for your game project, then reference it every time you prompt AI for art, audio, or visual assets. A completed style brief keeps your entire game looking and sounding like it was made by one team with one vision.

## How to Use This Brief

Once you've filled this out, reference it in your AI prompts:

> "Following my style brief: [paste relevant section]. Now create [specific asset]."

Or keep it as a persistent reference document and remind AI at the start of each session:

> "I'm working on [Game Title]. Here's my style brief for reference: [paste brief]. All assets should match this direction."

The more consistently you reference your brief, the more cohesive your game will look and sound.
